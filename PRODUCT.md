# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Undecided. No application code lives in this repository yet, and no build has
been requested. Ask once before building the first surface.

## Users

Confirmed by the ad brief: small business owners and teams who need ongoing
design work but find hiring a full-time designer too costly and too much
management overhead. They are comparing "hire a designer" against "subscribe
to a design team" on monthly cost and operational burden.

Open: company size, industry, and whether buyers are founders or marketers.

## Product Purpose

A monthly design subscription. One team covers a client's recurring design
needs so the client does not hire, onboard, or manage an in-house designer.

## Positioning

Fixed monthly price against a variable, higher total cost of employment. The
comparison the brief makes:

- Hiring: from ₩3,000,000/month, plus statutory insurance and severance,
  plus coverage gaps during leave, plus day-to-day direction and management.
- Subscribing: ₩1,990,000/month flat, none of the above, and one team that
  covers several design disciplines rather than one person's skill set.

## Operating Context

Acquisition runs through Meta (Facebook/Instagram) feed ads. Creatives are
produced at 1:1 (1080×1080) and 4:5 (1080×1350).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed delivery scope: branding, homepage, detail/landing pages, and
  packaging.
- Confirmed price: ₩1,990,000 per month, flat.
- This repository holds AI agent skills only — no `package.json`, `src/`, or
  entry point. Product code, if any, lives elsewhere.
- Undecided: contract length, revision limits, turnaround time, concurrent
  request limits, and what happens beyond the subscribed scope.

## Brand Commitments

- Point color `#fb923c`, pinned by the user.
- Light ground (white), chosen by the user over a dark treatment.

Recorded as given; neither has been expanded into a palette or a visual
system. `#fb923c` fails text contrast on white (2.1:1), so accent *text* in
the ad creative uses `#C2410C` while `#fb923c` carries fills. Any future
surface inherits that split rather than re-deciding it.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No customers, case studies, testimonials, portfolio pieces, benchmarks,
or shipped surfaces have been provided. The ₩3,000,000 hiring figure is the
brief's own comparison claim, not a sourced benchmark. Future work must not
fabricate proof.

## Product Principles

1. The offer is a cost and burden comparison; lead with the two numbers.
2. Every claim traces to something the client confirmed. No invented proof.
3. One team, several disciplines — breadth of coverage is the differentiator
   against a single hire.
